# Scientific decisions

This register lists the scientific and methodological choices of the project: what was chosen, why, and where the choice is owned. The linked guide explains it and the named config sets its values; change a value there, not here. A changed choice changes the identity it enters (design, Dataset, experiment or checkpoint) and needs its own comparison. Where VP1 did otherwise, the [corrigendum](Albertin_2026_PINO_Airflow_PorousMedia_corrigendum.md) point is named.

## Physical model and data generation

| Decision | Choice | Why | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Domain and grid | 2D bed 1.2 m × 0.75 m without resolved grains, boundary-inclusive grid of 251 rows (y) × 401 columns (x) | Grid and domain are physical model inputs; conversion admits only an export that covers the configured axes exactly | [Domain and resolution](02_physical_model.md#domain-and-resolution), `configs/01_generation/physical_model/common.yaml` (`grid`) |
| Transient horizon | 168 h with hourly output; a shorter horizon is part of the case identity, solver timeouts and walltimes are not; an irregular exact stop at the drying target is diagnostic evidence only, not regular training output | The horizon changes the physical output | [Domain and resolution](02_physical_model.md#domain-and-resolution), `common.yaml` (`time`) |
| Airflow model | Steady Darcy–Brinkman flow with heterogeneous `kappa(x)` and `epsilon(x)`, inlet pressure varying along the inlet edge y = 0, outlet gauge zero at y = L_y | Its `(p, u, v)` is the steady learning target and the fixed airflow of the transient profile | [Model: Airflow](02_physical_model.md#model) |
| Permeability–porosity coupling | Sampled permeability scale `kappa_mean` (log transform) sets the packing porosity through a material-calibrated Kozeny–Carman trend, shifted per case by a bounded packing scatter (σ = one third of the distance to the nearer support bound, z truncated to ±3) | Couples porosity to the permeability level while leaving room for unresolved morphology inside the packing support. VP1: porosity independent of the permeability level (corrigendum 15) | [Model: Airflow](02_physical_model.md#model), `registry.yaml` (`kappa_mean`), `materials/*.yaml` |
| Porosity field | Zero-mean texture from the background of the bed structure field, clipped to [0.2, 0.8]; κ and ε correlated through the shared background, not tied pointwise; a realization whose mean porosity leaves the natural support (or, for `kappa_mean`-OOD cases, crosses back over the natural bound) is redrawn | Keeps every case on its intended side of the natural support | [Model: Airflow](02_physical_model.md#model), `common.yaml` (`eps_min_global`, `eps_max_global`), `fixed_bed.yaml` (`parameter_values`: `porosity.*`; `constraints`: `porosity_natural_support_policy`) |
| Anisotropy | Local principal-permeability ratio r = 1 + strength·(max_ratio − 1)·\|b\|^exponent with \|b\| ≤ 1; principal permeabilities κ√r and κ/√r, so k₁/k₂ = r and their geometric mean is κ; `max_ratio` ID 1.2–2.2 (nominal 1.6), OOD 2.6–3.2 | `max_ratio` is the ratio its name states. Measured directional airflow resistance gives ratios of about 2 for lentil beds and 1.7–2.2 for shelled corn, and the anisotropy grows as grains become less spherical; the cited evidence gives no directional ratios for chickpea or kidney bean, so the range is transferred to all materials. VP1 used a stretch factor whose principal ratio was its square (corrigendum 16) | `registry.yaml` (`permeability.anisotropy.*`), `fixed_bed.yaml`, `sources.yaml` |
| Inlet pressure | Base level `pressure_bc.mean` times (1 + shape); the sinusoidal and Gaussian shape terms are not mean-free, so the base level is not the realised inlet mean | Describes the profile as generated (corrigendum 20) | `registry.yaml` (`pressure_bc.*`), `fixed_bed.yaml` |
| Grain moisture and sorption | Two-compartment grain moisture (surface and interior) with an evaporation sink; Oswin isotherm per material; dry bulk density follows the realised packing | Material-level evidence where it exists; the kinetic priors are aligned with a limited numerical pilot, not fitted to experiments, and are no drying-time predictions (`limitation` in each material record) | [Model: Transient state](02_physical_model.md#model), [Parameters and evidence](02_physical_model.md#parameters-and-evidence), `materials/*.yaml` |
| Initial state and heat | Initial moisture drawn on a dry basis from a material-specific support, both compartments start at ρ_bu,dry·X₀, `T_init` equals the ambient temperature and the initial humidity is the inverse Oswin equilibrium; heat capacity includes the grain water, conductivity is the Maxwell–Eucken mixture of grain and air, evaporation removes latent heat (−h_fg·m_evap) | The bed starts in sorption equilibrium with its own moisture | [Model: Transient state](02_physical_model.md#model), `common.yaml` (`physical_formulas`), `registry.yaml` (`T_init`), `materials/*.yaml` (`initial_moisture`) |
| Drying target | Practical safe-storage moisture per material (lentil, chickpea, field pea 12 % wb; kidney bean 14 %; rapeseed, sunflower seed 6 %); the stop criterion compares the dry-mass fraction above target with 5 % | The rounded storage moisture is the practical target; the exact value stays in provenance | [Model: Drying target](02_physical_model.md#model), `materials/*.yaml` (`target_moisture`), `common.yaml` |
| Inlet schedule | Heater-only operation that keeps the humidity ratio of the source air; temperature and humidity ratio are interpolated linearly and the relative humidity is derived from them; canonical bounds T 290.15–313.15 K, ω 0.0025–0.0145 kg/kg, RH 0.05–0.85; a schedule whose derived humidity is infeasible anywhere is resampled as a whole, never clipped; a 0.5 h startup ramp from `T_init` whose humidity is the stricter of a 0.05 margin below the initial bed equilibrium and a 0.90 ceiling | Clipping would change the sampled distribution silently | [Model: Inlet schedule](02_physical_model.md#model), `common.yaml` (`T_in_min`, `T_in_max`, `omega_min`, `omega_max`, `phi_operational_min`, `phi_operational_max`, `schedule_interpolation`), `fixed_bed.yaml` (`constraints`, `boundary_schedule`) |
| Field statistics | One shared synthetic random-field statistic for all materials (open option D1 below) | No measured material-specific spatial priors are available | [Parameters and evidence](02_physical_model.md#parameters-and-evidence), `registry.yaml`, `fixed_bed.yaml` |
| Design | Latin hypercube per block (50 scrambled candidates, the one with the largest minimum nearest-neighbour distance kept); a changed count changes the whole design, not a prefix | Space-filling design for small counts | [Sampling](02_physical_model.md#sampling) |
| In and out of distribution | Natural support is ID; parameter OOD moves one eligible scalar or one complete coupled record outside it; material identity is provenance, not a model input | OOD stays interpretable per moved coordinate | [Sampling](02_physical_model.md#sampling), `fixed_bed.yaml` (`ood` of each entry in `parameter_values`), `registry.yaml` (`ood_group`), `materials/*.yaml` (`ood_supports`, `ood_records`) |

| Counts and split | Steady 500 + 500 + 150 OOD, split 0.8 / 0.1 / 0.1 per ID material; transient 100 + 100 + 30 OOD, split 0.7 / 0.15 / 0.15 per ID material, applied before temporal expansion | Steady Train 800 matches the VP1 training size; transient validation and ID-test are larger because selection rolls out whole trajectories and the test reports paired per-case differences | [The maintained plans](03_datasets.md#the-maintained-plans) |
| Fixed partition | The split seed (9) and the design order fix the split with the Dataset; run seeds never repartition it; ID-test and OOD stay out of fitting, pruning, checkpoint selection and the objective | One held-out ID estimate per Dataset. VP1 had no ID test partition and evaluated training cases (corrigendum 1) | [The maintained plans](03_datasets.md#the-maintained-plans), [Optuna and tracking](04_training.md#optuna-and-tracking) |
| Failed solves | Each terminal failure is replaced while the material's one-sided 95 % Clopper–Pearson upper bound of the success rate stays at or above the minimum success rate (steady 0.9, transient 0.6); the Dataset describes the solver-success population and reports its failures; a replacement is the next prefix-stable Sobol point of a separate seed stream, ordered after the originals, so it does not restore the Latin hypercube coverage | Replacement cannot undo the selection by success, so it is reported, not hidden | [Failed cases and replacements](03_datasets.md#failed-cases-and-replacements) |

VP1 fitted on all ID cases (corrigendum 1) | [Steady flow](04_training.md#steady-flow), [Inputs, scaling and grid](04_training.md#inputs-scaling-and-grid), [Runs and publication](07_reproducibility_and_validation.md#runs-and-publication) |

## Steady training and physics loss

| Decision | Choice | Why | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Data loss | `relative_h1` in normalized space, NeuralOp's relative norm per case and field averaged over both (`reduction="mean"`), with finite differences at the physical grid spacing in metres, one-sided at the non-periodic boundaries | Loss weights independent of the batch size; no periodic wrap across inlet and outlet; derivatives weighted as in the evaluation `rel_h1`. VP1: periodic stencil and batch sum (corrigendum 5, 8) | [Steady flow](04_training.md#steady-flow), `configs/03_learning/steady_flow/base.yaml` (`loss.data`) |
| Physics terms | Three dimensionless terms, each with its own weight and linear warm-up: momentum, cross-section flux balance, boundary (inlet mismatch plus squared outlet mean per case); scales from the inputs only | Each term weighted on its own, in comparable units. VP1: one unscaled sum and a batch-pooled outlet (corrigendum 8, 9) | [Steady flow](04_training.md#steady-flow), PI experiment configs (`loss.physics`) |
| Viscous stress | COMSOL's Brinkman stress (μ/ε)(∇u + ∇uᵀ − ⅔(∇·u)I), trace term kept | Same stress as the solver that produced the reference (corrigendum 13) | [Steady flow](04_training.md#steady-flow) |
| Continuity | Flux balance through each row instead of pointwise ∇·u or ∇·(εu) | The interpolated COMSOL solution is not pointwise divergence-free on the grid, while its flux balance nearly vanishes (corrigendum 14) | [Steady flow](04_training.md#steady-flow) |
| Starting weights | Momentum 1, continuity 10³, boundary 10², warm-up 100 epochs; the PI studies search the three weights two decades around these values | Each term comparable to the data loss at about 1 % field error | [Steady flow](04_training.md#steady-flow), [Optuna and tracking](04_training.md#optuna-and-tracking) |
| Derivatives and crop | The maintained physics configs use `physical` (finite-difference) derivatives with `interior_crop: 2` on the momentum term; `spectral` always uses Fourier continuation | The Brinkman wall layer is far below the grid spacing, so the uncropped wall columns would dominate the momentum term of the exact solution; the bed is not periodic (corrigendum 2, 6) | [Steady flow](04_training.md#steady-flow), PI experiment configs (`loss.physics.derivatives`, `interior_crop`) |
| Objective | `normalized_group_macro_rmse` over the pressure and velocity groups, accumulated over the complete validation set; drives checkpoint, scheduler and studies | Dimensionless, with equal weight for the pressure and the velocity group. VP1: batch-summed RMSE in mixed units (corrigendum 3) | [Steady flow](04_training.md#steady-flow), `steady_flow/base.yaml` (`evaluation.objective`) |
| Optimization | AdamW with weight decay 10⁻⁴; learning rate halved on a plateau of 20 validation checks, down to 10⁻⁵; up to 600 epochs with validation every second epoch; batch size 32; float32 without mixed precision | Defaults outside the study search spaces; half-precision cuFFT needs power-of-two grids (251 × 401 is not) and finite-difference physics derivatives lose their accuracy in half precision | `steady_flow/base.yaml` (`optimizer`, `scheduler`, `training`, `data`) |
| Architectures | FNO and U-NO with physics-informed twins of identical architecture and learning rate; mode counts ordered [Y, X], the U-NO mode ratio stated in full; U-NO lifting and projection widths 2 × hidden channels like the FNO | Twins isolate the effect of the physics loss (corrigendum 22 for the axis order); neuraloperator's fixed 256 would not scale with the width | [Configurations](04_training.md#configurations), `steady_flow/experiments/architecture_comparison/` |
| Coordinates and padding | The seven input channels carry `x` and `y` explicitly, so `positional_embedding: null`; `domain_padding: 0.1` (10 % zero padding per side of the lifted field) | The grid embedding is periodic without endpoint and would give one physical point different coordinates at different strides; the fields are not periodic, so padding keeps the FFT wrap-around away from the boundaries | [Configurations](04_training.md#configurations), `steady_flow/base.yaml` (`model.params`) |

## Transient training

| Decision | Choice | Why | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Protocol | Matched A0 → {B, A1}: teacher-forced A0, then autonomous B and teacher-forced A1, both from A0's selected checkpoint with its full training state; B trains for the measured optimization time of the sweep A0 on the same GPU type, fixed per architecture for all seeds, and A1 for B's actual seconds, which it may exceed by at most one optimizer group; each stage also reports its optimizer steps and gradient predictions | Compares training protocols at matched compute | [Matched comparison](04_training.md#matched-comparison-a0--b-a1), `configs/03_learning/transient_drying/base.yaml` (`training`) |
| Rollout curriculum | B rolls out 2 → 4 → 8 → 16 → 32 steps at budget fractions 0, 0.2, 0.4, 0.6, 0.8 in windows of 32 transitions; windows start every 32 transitions, plus one that ends at the trajectory end, and B's origins rotate over the three thirds of the window | Grows the autonomous horizon gradually | [Matched comparison](04_training.md#matched-comparison-a0--b-a1), `base.yaml` |
| Checkpoint selection | Every stage and architecture selects by `normalized_drying_group_macro_rmse` (T and φ 1/3, w_surf and w_int 1/6) on autonomous rollouts of whole validation trajectories, pooled over all predicted states; A0 validates every epoch, B and A1 at the epoch ends that cross one of 20 equally spaced budget points | One rule for every stage and architecture, measured on autonomous prediction | [Losses and objective](04_training.md#losses-and-objective) |
| Loss | Huber (β = 1) on scaled increments with equal channel weights (open option D3 below); no physics loss | Robust loss on the predicted increments; the transient physics loss is disabled in the current program | [Losses and objective](04_training.md#losses-and-objective), `base.yaml` (`loss`) |
| Inputs | 28 ordered channels with explicit coordinates and no normalized-time channel (open option D2 below); training on every second grid point (126 × 201) | The endpoint-preserving stride keeps both boundaries in the training grid; artifacts use the full grid | [Inputs, scaling and grid](04_training.md#inputs-scaling-and-grid), `base.yaml` (`data`) |
| Coordinates and padding | Explicit `x` and `y` channels, so `positional_embedding: null`; `domain_padding: 0.1` for FNO, U-NO and RNO (10 % zero padding per side of the lifted field; the RNO carries its hidden state padded) | The grid embedding would give one physical point different coordinates at stride 2 and stride 1; the fields are not periodic, so padding keeps the FFT wrap-around away from the boundaries | [Configurations](04_training.md#configurations), `transient_drying/base.yaml` (`model.params`) |
| RNO | `tanh` candidate cell instead of neuraloperator's `selu`; truncated backpropagation through time in windows of 32, each starting from the detached hidden state the preceding window reached; in B a teacher-forced pass without gradient provides the state at each origin | SELU is unbounded and the self-fed rollout diverged; `tanh` keeps the hidden state bounded | [Matched comparison](04_training.md#matched-comparison-a0--b-a1), `rno_m24x24_h16_l3.yaml` |
| Optimization | AdamW with weight decay 10⁻⁴; learning rate halved on a plateau of 10 validation checks, down to 10⁻⁶; A0 up to 100 epochs; batch size 2; float32 without mixed precision | Defaults outside the study search spaces; half-precision cuFFT needs power-of-two grids (126 × 201 is not) | `transient_drying/base.yaml` (`optimizer`, `scheduler`, `training`, `data`) |
| Continuation rate | B and A1 continue with `lr_scale` 1; any other rate is chosen by a sweep with a grid and tie rule declared before it and before any test evaluation | Same scales for both arms from the same A0 and budget | [Continuation sweep](04_training.md#continuation-sweep-for-b-and-a1) |

| Decision | Choice | Why | Owner |

## Evaluation

| Decision | Choice | Why | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Steady case metrics | Per field in physical units: `rel_l2` on the full grid and `rel_h1` with central differences in metres on the interior without `EVAL_PAD = 2` samples, each averaged over the fields; RMSE per field, velocity vector and speed | Every field counts equally. This `rel_h1` is a metric in metres, not the training loss on the unit square. VP1: one undefined norm over concatenated fields (corrigendum 3) | [Steady case metrics](05_evaluation.md#steady-case-metrics) |
| Physics yardstick | Every steady run's physics terms are re-evaluated with physical finite differences, momentum on the `EVAL_PAD` interior, whatever derivative it was trained with; reference fields pass the same functions | One yardstick for all runs. VP1: different crops and drag for models and reference (corrigendum 6, 7) | [Steady case metrics](05_evaluation.md#steady-case-metrics), [Physics diagnostics](05_evaluation.md#physics-diagnostics) |
| Transient protocols | Teacher-forced one-step, full autonomous rollout and rolling origins; horizons 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64 and 128 transitions plus full, each with cumulative and endpoint reductions; a horizon longer than a case is recorded as unsupported; the RNO's one-step records and rolling origins start from the state of a teacher-forced pass over the reference prefix, its full rollout from its own state | Separates one-step accuracy from error growth | [Transient protocols and metrics](05_evaluation.md#transient-protocols-and-metrics) |
| Validity | Predictions are never clamped, filtered or imputed; a transient state is physically invalid when T leaves [0, 2000] K, φ leaves [0, 1.10] or a moisture field is negative, with the 2 × 2 corner blocks of every channel excluded from that count but not from the non-finite count; an autonomous rollout stops before feeding an invalid state back; steady cases are valid or non-finite | The φ tolerance admits the slight supersaturation of the reference itself, an accepted model limit to re-check on production data | [Prediction validity](05_evaluation.md#transient-protocols-and-metrics) |
| Incomplete support | A metric that needs complete finite support reports its required, computed, finite and non-finite counts instead of a value and is never averaged over the valid cases alone | A mean over the finite cases alone would hide the failed cases | [Prediction validity](05_evaluation.md#transient-protocols-and-metrics) |
| Comparisons | Only runs with the same task, Dataset membership, objective, protocol, artifact grid and analysis producer are compared; transient runs are ranked by the equal-material mean of the cumulative full-autonomous objective; B against A1 is the protocol comparison at matched compute, B against A0 includes the additional training | Only like is compared with like | [Comparisons](05_evaluation.md#comparisons) |
| Pipeline and speed | The pipeline view separates the drying-surrogate error (surrogate on COMSOL airflow), the complete-pipeline error (surrogate on predicted airflow) and the airflow substitution between them; paired speedups aggregate as summed reference time over summed surrogate time, and speedups that combine different measurement boundaries are labelled component-composed | Attributes errors and speedups to their source | [Comparisons](05_evaluation.md#comparisons), [Timing and speedup](05_evaluation.md#timing-and-speedup) |
| Evaluation grid | Artifacts on the full 251 × 401 grid by default, independent of the training stride | Metrics on the generated resolution | [Evaluation grid](05_evaluation.md#evaluation-grid) |

## Reproducibility

| Decision | Choice | Why | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Seeds | Generation seed base 9200 (transient) and 9300 (steady); `run.seed` 9 as the only learning seed; replicates 9, 19 and 29; every study seed 9 | Independent streams for design, split and training | [Seeds](../configs/README.md#seeds) |
| Determinism | `deterministic: false`; seeds fix initialization and data order, not the bits | Strict deterministic CUDA rejects kernels the transient models use | [Matched comparison](04_training.md#matched-comparison-a0--b-a1) |

| Evidence | Fake-solver and numerical checks establish wiring only; physical validity and scientific acceptance need their own evidence | Technical checks are no physical validation | [Choosing checks](07_reproducibility_and_validation.md#choosing-checks) |

## Open choices

| Question | Current default | Owner of the default |
| --- | --- | --- |
